# Plugly Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Plugly is published by SHOT Clubhouse Ltd ("we", "us"). This policy covers the Plugly desktop app and the Plugly mobile app (Android package `com.pluglyhq.plugly.mobile`). Contact: steve@shotclubhouse.com.

## Summary

- Plugly has no account system. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or payment details.
- The mobile app talks to your own paired desktop. Terminal content, prompts, files and repository data stay between your phone and your computer and are not sent to us.
- We collect limited, anonymous diagnostics and a push notification token. We do not sell data and do not use it for advertising.

## What the mobile app does with your data

- **Camera.** Used only to scan the QR code that pairs your phone with your desktop. Images are processed on the device and are not stored or uploaded.
- **Microphone.** Used only for voice dictation. Audio is sent to your own paired desktop for transcription, not to us.
- **Photos.** Images you choose to attach are sent to your own paired desktop session.
- **Push notifications.** To deliver notifications we handle a push token and a random device identifier through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and our push gateway. The token is used only to deliver notifications you have enabled.
- **Diagnostics and usage.** When enabled, we receive anonymous product-usage events keyed by a random identifier stored on your device: app version, operating system, device platform and coarse behaviour such as which features are used. We never receive file contents, prompts, agent or terminal output, repository names, paths, URLs or commit messages. You can turn this off in Settings, Privacy.

## What the desktop app collects

The desktop app sends the same kind of anonymous usage events described above (no content, no file paths, no IP address stored against a profile). It can be disabled in Settings, Privacy, or by setting `DO_NOT_TRACK=1` or `PLUGLY_TELEMETRY_DISABLED=1`.

## Where data goes

- Anonymous usage analytics: PostHog Cloud, United States region.
- Push delivery: Google Firebase Cloud Messaging.
- Connections between your phone and your desktop are encrypted in transit.

## Sharing

We do not sell personal data and do not share it with third parties except the service providers listed above, who process it on our behalf.

## Retention and deletion

Analytics data is kept for the retention period of our analytics provider. To have data tied to your device removed, email steve@shotclubhouse.com from the address you want us to reply to and describe your device; because events use a random identifier, we may ask you to supply that identifier from the app. Uninstalling the app removes the locally stored identifier and push token registration.

## Children

Plugly is not directed at children under 13 and is intended for adults.

## Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the date above.
